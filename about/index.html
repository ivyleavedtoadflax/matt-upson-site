About

I’ve spent the last decade building AI systems and leading teams in government, not-for-profits, and startups. I co-founded Mantis NLP, an AI consultancy, and served as CEO and then CTO over five years. I now work independently with organisations who need senior AI capability without the overhead of a full-time hire.

The career arc

  1. Research Fellow & PhD ResearcherCranfield University, modelling forest growth and soil carbon storage
  2. Data ScientistDepartment for Education (first data scientist recruited to the department)
  3. Data ScientistGovernment Digital Service (GDS), pioneered Reproducible Analytical Pipelines
  4. Lead Data ScientistJuro, AI-enabled contract management platform
  5. Freelance Data ScientistReproducible Analytics, clients including the Wellcome Trust
  6. Founder & CEO, then CTOMantis NLP, built to profitability over 5 years
  7. Consultant (Network Member)Public Digital, digital transformation for governments
  8. Independent AI ConsultantBased in Valencia, Spain

The human stuff

I live in Valencia, Spain with my family. When I’m not working, I’m usually training for a triathlon, running along the Turia, or trying to improve my Spanish. My career path was unusual: I started as an environmental scientist, retrained in data science, and found my way to AI via government. That non-linear route means I bring a different perspective to most AI consultants; I understand research rigour, public interest constraints, and the gap between academic AI and production systems.
